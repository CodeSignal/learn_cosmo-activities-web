__Practice Question__

A goblin reorganized your notes app and **bolded** everything at random. Sort each snippet by whether it is written in `inline code` or **bold** Markdown.

- `Inline code`

__Items__

- **Do not feed the gremlins**: **Bold** text
- `console.log("boo")`: `Inline code`
